About Our Church

A Community Built on God's Word

Olives Fellowship Temple has been serving the Kigali community for over 35 years. We are a diverse, welcoming congregation committed to spreading the love of Christ through worship, fellowship, and outreach.

Our mission is simple: to know God, grow in faith, and make a difference in the lives of others. Whether you're a lifelong believer or exploring faith for the first time, you are welcome here.
